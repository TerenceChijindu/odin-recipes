Creamy Cajun-Spiced Swamp Cabbage

Description

This creamy Cajun-spiced swamp cabbage combines smoky andouille sausage and tender cabbage in a rich Parmesan cream sauce with bold Cajun seasoning. Ready in about 30 minutes, it is an out-of-this-world side dish that just might change the way you make cabbage.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 pound andouille sausage, sliced
  • 1 medium yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning, or more to taste
  • 1 small green cabbage, chopped into 1 inch pieces
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add sausage and cook until ends begin to turn brown, about 5 minutes. Mix in yellow onion and reduce heat to medium. Season with Cajun seasoning to taste and continue cooking until onion begins to soften and sausage is browned, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add cabbage and continue cooking until edges begin to brown, 5 to 10 minutes.
  3. Add chicken stock and continue cooking until liquid has reduced by about half, about 5 minutes. Stir in heavy cream and Parmesan cheese and continue cooking until mixture begins to thicken, about 5 minutes.
